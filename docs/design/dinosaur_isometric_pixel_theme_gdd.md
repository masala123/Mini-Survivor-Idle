# Dinosaur Isometric Pixel Theme - World & Theme Design

## High Concept

A modern human survivor is suddenly transported into a dangerous prehistoric world filled with dinosaurs, mysterious ruins, and dimensional anomalies.

The survivor must:
- survive
- adapt
- build infrastructure
- uncover the mystery
- eventually find a way home

The player acts as an invisible guiding force helping the survivor survive.

---

# Theme Formula

```text
Retro Pixel Art
+
2.5D Isometric Survival Sandbox
+
Dinosaur Ecosystem Simulation
+
Autonomous Survivor AI
+
Dimensional Mystery Narrative
```

---

# Visual Direction

## Art Style

- Retro pixel art
- SNES/PS1-era inspiration
- Strong silhouettes
- Atmospheric environments
- Stylized prehistoric nature

## Camera Style

```text
2.5D Isometric
```

Recommended:
- 2:1 isometric ratio
- diagonal terrain readability
- layered environments
- fake depth

# Gameplay Theme Pillars

## Primitive Survival

Early gameplay focuses on:
- food
- fire
- shelter
- escaping predators
- surviving night

## Dinosaur Ecosystem

Dinosaurs are ecosystem actors, not generic enemies.

Examples:
- migration
- hunting
- territorial behavior
- scavenging
- nesting
- stampedes

## Lost Civilization Mystery

The world contains:
- ruins
- ancient machines
- portal fragments
- dimensional artifacts

## Human Adaptation

The survivor evolves from:

```text
Lost human
→ Primitive survivor
→ Builder
→ Explorer
→ Dimensional traveler
```
