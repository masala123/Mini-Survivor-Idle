# Design Pillars

## Pillar 1 — Autonomous Survival

The survivor should feel alive independently.

## Pillar 2 — Meaningful Assistance

The player helps survival without replacing it.

## Pillar 3 — Escalating Threats

The world becomes increasingly dangerous over time.

## Pillar 4 — Emergent Storytelling

Unexpected survival situations create memorable stories.

## Pillar 5 — Infrastructure Automation

Progression evolves from survival to optimization.
