# Base Building System

## Base Philosophy

The base is the central hub for survival logistics and automated processing. It evolves from a simple camp to a fortified dimensional bridge.

## Structure Categories

### 1. Survival & Utility
| Structure | Purpose | Interaction |
|---|---|---|
| **Campfire** | Night safety, Morale boost | Survivors relax nearby at night |
| **Workbench** | Crafting center | Enables advanced recipe crafting |
| **Chest** | General storage | Manual or automatic item deposit |

### 2. Food & Production
| Structure | Purpose | Interaction |
|---|---|---|
| **Farm Plot** | Sustainable food | Grows vegetables (faster in rain) |

### 3. Advanced Logistics & Stockpiles
| Structure | Purpose | Specialized For |
|---|---|---|
| **Wood Shed** | Specialized storage | Wood only |
| **Stone Mason** | Specialized storage | Stone, Bones, Fossils |

### 4. Automated Processing
| Structure | Purpose | Input / Output |
|---|---|---|
| **Stone Furnace** | Material refining | Fossil + Wood → Refined Fossil |

### 5. Defensive Infrastructure
| Structure | Purpose | Stats |
|---|---|---|
| **Wall** | Basic obstruction | Low HP |
| **Bone Wall** | Advanced obstruction | High HP |
| **Spike Trap** | Active defense | Damage hostiles on contact |

### 6. Endgame / Victory
| Structure | Purpose | Ingredients |
|---|---|---|
| **Dimensional Beacon** | Portal to victory | Ancient Tech, Refined Fossils |

## Automation & AI Logistics

Survivors automatically manage the base through the **HAUL** and **REFINERY** tasks:
- **Hauling**: AI moves materials from generic chests to specialized stockpiles.
- **Processing**: AI supplies furnaces with raw materials and hauls finished products to storage.
- **Construction**: AI prioritizes building blueprints during the afternoon.
